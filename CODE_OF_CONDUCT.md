# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome questions and different levels of experience. Discuss code and evidence without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and sharing private information are unacceptable.

This community follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) for expected behavior and enforcement guidance. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, and explain moderation decisions where privacy permits.

For a conduct concern, contact a maintainer through a private channel offered on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/aunysillyme). Keep personal details out of public issues. Security vulnerabilities use [private vulnerability reporting](SECURITY.md).
